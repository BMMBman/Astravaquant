Privacy notice.

Privacy

Read-only by design.

This notice explains what AstravaQuant processes when you browse public research and, where applicable, authenticate a wallet for private portfolio context.

Effective September 3, 2026

Information We Process

Public pages can be browsed without authentication. If a user connects a wallet on the private portfolio tooling, AstravaQuant processes the public wallet address, selected chain, authentication nonce, session identifiers, and login timing.

What We Store

AstravaQuant stores the wallet-to-user relationship, session records, and periodic total-value snapshots used to build real portfolio history. It does not need private keys, seed phrases, or transaction approval data.

Analytics

Aggregate usage analytics may be used to understand the public research product. Wallet addresses, balances, holdings, signatures, and nonces are excluded from analytics properties.

Third-Party Providers

Public research pages may use external data and infrastructure providers such as Google Sheets, CoinGecko, FRED, Vercel, Neon, and other read-only services. Those providers operate under their own terms.

Security Boundary

AstravaQuant does not request or store private keys or seed phrases and does not execute trades or move assets. Authentication signatures prove wallet control only.

Changes

This notice may change as the research platform evolves. The effective date above identifies the current published version.

AstravaQuant is public research first and authenticated tooling second.
