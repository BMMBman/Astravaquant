About

Independent systematic market research.

AstravaQuant studies market regimes across liquidity, crypto, interest rates, valuation, and macro conditions using transparent quantitative frameworks.

What It Is

An independent quantitative research platform.

  • Systematic model publication.
  • Methodology disclosure where available.
  • Dated historical series and research notes.

What It Is Not

  • Not a crypto trading platform.
  • Not a brokerage or institutional asset manager.
  • Not an AI trading bot or Bloomberg Terminal imitation.

Publication Standard

The public site should become more credible the deeper someone goes: homepage simple, models clear, methodology detailed, backtests auditable, research written and sourced.

Disclosure Boundary

Where exact formulas, weights, thresholds, or historical procedures are not available in the current repo, the site uses clearly marked placeholders instead of invented detail.

Sophistication should come from depth, not visual effects.
