03 / Interpretation

Why liquidity belongs in the market map.

It is context for financial conditions—not proof of causation, a return forecast, or an allocation instruction.

01

Balance-sheet capacity

Federal Reserve assets can add system liquidity, while Treasury cash balances and reverse repo usage can absorb it. Netting them provides a more useful backdrop than reading any one series alone.

02

Direction over headline

The weekly and four-week change show whether the backdrop is expanding or contracting. The level can be large while the marginal impulse is moving the other way.

03

Context, not causation

Liquidity can help frame risk conditions, but assets respond to many variables. AstravaQuant does not treat this series as a stand-alone timing model or performance claim.

04 / Construction

Public inputs. Explicit arithmetic.

All inputs are normalized to millions of U.S. dollars before alignment. Overnight reverse repo is converted from billions. BTFP is retained historically and set to zero after discontinuation.

Net Fed Liquidity WALCL - WTREGEN - (RRPONTSYD × 1,000) + H41RESPPALDKNWW + WLCFLPCL

Federal Reserve assets minus the Treasury General Account and overnight reverse repo, plus BTFP and primary credit.

Source: Federal Reserve H.4.1 data via FRED. The series is updated on the source release schedule. No substitute reading is shown when the feed is unavailable.
