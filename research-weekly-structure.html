What LTPI measures

LTPI is the public long-term trend model. It combines total crypto market capitalization with the Bitcoin chart on a weekly horizon.

The slower timeframe is meant to describe structural direction rather than the faster participation state captured by MTPI.

How to read it

LTPI should be read as a structural condition, not a guarantee. Its score uses the shared crypto regime scale and is best interpreted alongside MTPI and the derived NSPI.

Different horizons can disagree. That disagreement is information rather than automatic model failure.

Interpretation LTPI is the slower structural read; MTPI shows whether faster participation agrees.

Disclosure boundary

The current public repo discloses inputs, source tabs, dated observations, and interpretation bands. Exact transformations, weights, and the acronym expansion remain unpublished.
