Regime Scale

How MTPI, LTPI, and NSPI are read.

These three models share the same minus one to plus one regime scale.

-1.00 to -0.75 Extreme risk-off
-0.75 to -0.25 Defensive contraction
-0.25 to +0.25 Neutral transition
+0.25 to +0.75 Constructive expansion
+0.75 to +1.00 Full risk-on
MRPI Scale

MRPI is read differently.

MRPI is not a risk-on scale. Negative reads mean tightening on the 10-year backdrop. Positive reads mean easing.

-1.00 to -0.50 Tightening pressure
-0.50 to +0.25 Mixed transition
+0.25 to +1.00 Easing pressure
