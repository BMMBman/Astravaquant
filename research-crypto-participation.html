The model

What MTPI measures

MTPI is AstravaQuant's medium-term crypto trend model. It aggregates the total crypto market capitalization and TOTAL2 on a five-day timeframe.

Total market capitalization captures the broad crypto tape. TOTAL2 removes Bitcoin, adding context on participation beyond the market's largest asset.
