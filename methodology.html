Methodology

How the models are built, tested, and interpreted.

AstravaQuant uses systematic frameworks designed to convert market data into repeatable measurements of trend, liquidity, valuation, and regime.

The objective is not to predict every market movement. The objective is to define market conditions consistently and evaluate how those definitions have behaved historically.

01 / Trend Following

Trend

The current public trend stack consists of a five-day medium-term model and a weekly long-term model. Both use shared regime bands on a normalized `-1.00` to `+1.00` scale.

02 / Mortgage Rate Pressure

Mortgage Rate Pressure

MRPI is a separate weekly system anchored to the 10-year Treasury backdrop. It is interpreted as tightening versus easing pressure rather than risk-on versus risk-off.

Open MRPI

03 / Liquidity / Regime

Liquidity / Regime

The current public regime composite is NSPI, which the application derives as the mean of the latest published MTPI and LTPI values at each dated update. No broader liquidity formula is disclosed in the current repo.

04 / Valuation

Valuation

The current valuation system averages 17 standardized input readings across fundamental, technical, and sentiment categories. The app publishes the workbook score without converting it into a dollar target.

Open Valuation

Backtesting

Test the rule, not the story.

AstravaQuant evaluates historical behavior using dated score observations that already exist in the published workbooks. The public app does not fabricate return series, fill missing dates, or imply live track records from hypothetical simulations.
