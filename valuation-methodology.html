Valuation.

The current valuation system uses standardized measurements to determine how far current conditions sit from their historical distribution.

Valuation answers a different question than trend or regime: it describes relative historical condition rather than short-term direction or broader environmental state.

Objective Publish a standardized value score built from fundamental, technical, and sentiment inputs without turning that score into a fair-value price target.
Implementation The public app reads a 17-input workbook, computes the arithmetic mean of the published z-scores, and compares it with the workbook’s composite.
Categories Seven fundamental inputs, seven technical inputs, and three sentiment inputs.
Primary Scale The score is oriented so positive values mean greater modeled value in the current workbook. The inverse lens multiplies that score by `-1`.
FundamentalBTC: Supply in Profit Market Bands; Short-Term Holder MVRV Indicator; True Market Mean Price + AVIV Ratio; NUPL; Adjusted MVRV; Puell Multiple; MVRV Pricing Bands.
Technical52-Week BTC Sharpe Ratio; BTC RSI; The Mayer Multiple; BTC SOPR Z-Score; BTC Short-Term Holder Supply P/L Ratio; BTC STH Cost Basis Delta; Bitcoin Macro Oscillator (BMO).
SentimentCrypto Fear & Greed Index; Google Trends (BTC - 5 Years); Sentix BTC Sentiment.
Normalization Each input is expressed as a Z-score: its distance from the relevant historical mean in standard deviations. The app publishes the composite on the workbook’s declared scale.
Historical Series Forward-testing history appears only when the workbook contains dated score observations. The app does not backfill missing history.
Classification The workbook publishes the current state label. The public application does not hard-code expensive, neutral, and cheap thresholds beyond reading the workbook output.
Update Frequency The valuation workbook is re-read every five minutes by default.
Methodology change log Changes to valuation inputs, normalization windows, classifications, or composite construction are versioned in the public methodology register before publication.
Disclaimer Valuation is educational market research, not investment advice, a price target, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold Bitcoin. Z-scores can remain elevated or depressed and past readings do not predict future outcomes.

Valuation should be standardized and inspectable, not overstated.
