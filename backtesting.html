Backtesting Methodology

What AstravaQuant evaluates.

Data

  • Public backtests use dated score rows already present in the connected workbooks.
  • Blank rows, future placeholders, and formula errors are excluded rather than coerced to zero.
  • Source tabs are disclosed alongside each series.

Signal

  • Series include MTPI, LTPI, derived NSPI, MRPI, and the Bitcoin valuation composite.
  • Thresholds are adjustable for inspection, but the dated scores themselves are not altered.
  • Derived NSPI uses the latest available MTPI and LTPI value at each update date.

Execution Assumptions

  • The public app does not convert score changes into assumed trades, fills, or P&L.
  • Closing-data timing, slippage, fees, and transaction costs remain undisclosed unless a benchmark test is added.
  • No backtest on this page should be read as realized performance.

Test Design

  • Observed history is limited to the dated rows published in the forward-testing sheets.
  • In-sample, out-of-sample, and walk-forward procedures are not fully disclosed in the current repo.
Revision Control Material parameter, threshold, and methodology changes are logged before a revised public backtest is published.

Robustness

  • The current public page supports threshold and window inspection.
  • Alternative lookbacks, sensitivity analysis, and subperiod studies are not yet published.

Limitations

  • Hypothetical analysis is hypothetical.
  • Look-ahead bias, overfitting, data-mining, and regime dependence must be discussed explicitly.
  • Past performance does not guarantee future results.
