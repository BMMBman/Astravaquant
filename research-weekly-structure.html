The model

What LTPI measures

LTPI is AstravaQuant's long-term crypto trend model. It combines the total crypto market capitalization with the Bitcoin chart on a weekly timeframe.

The slower timeframe is intended to define structural direction rather than the faster participation state captured by MTPI.

Current state

Weekly structure remains risk-off

The published LTPI value is -0.95. On the shared crypto regime scale, that sits inside Extreme Risk-Off and carries a Short state.

This does not erase the constructive +0.47 MTPI reading. The disagreement between fast and slow models is exactly why NSPI is currently near neutral rather than fully directional.

InterpretationMedium-term participation has improved, but the weekly structure has not confirmed it.
