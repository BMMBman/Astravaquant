What MRPI measures

MRPI is the Mortgage Rate Pressure Index. It is a weekly framework designed to measure directional pressure in mortgage-rate conditions via the 10-year Treasury backdrop.

Unlike MTPI, LTPI, and NSPI, MRPI should not be described as risk-on or risk-off. Negative readings indicate tightening pressure; positive readings indicate easing pressure.

How to read it

The published score describes regime pressure, not a direct prediction of mortgage rates or an instruction to transact. It is meant to be read beside public rates and housing context.
