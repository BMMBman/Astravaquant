Methodology / Trend

Trend following.

Trend models attempt to systematically identify the direction and persistence of market movement rather than forecast an exact future price.

AstravaQuant maintains multiple horizons because trend behavior can differ materially depending on the measurement window.

Long-Term Trend

Purpose Measures slower structural crypto trend on a weekly horizon.
Inputs Total crypto market capitalization, the Bitcoin chart, the `LTPI` workbook summary, and the `LT Total Forward Testing` dated series.
Signal Construction The public repo discloses the inputs and dated outputs, but not the exact transformation stack, weighting, or acronym expansion behind LTPI.
Placeholder For Troy Supply the exact long-term signal construction, weighting, and the intended public LTPI definition if it should be fully expanded.
Normalization The public app reads the workbook score on a normalized `-1.00` to `+1.00` scale and applies the same regime bands used by MTPI.
Regime Thresholds `-1.00 to -0.75` Extreme Risk-Off, `-0.75 to -0.25` Defensive Contraction, `-0.25 to +0.25` Neutral Transition, `+0.25 to +0.75` Constructive Expansion, `+0.75 to +1.00` Full Risk-On.
Update Frequency The public site refreshes the workbook feed every five minutes by default and carries through the worksheet’s published update label when present.
Historical Test Period The public site uses dated rows from `LT Total Forward Testing` only.
Current Interpretation LTPI is the slow structural read in the public stack and is intended to be read beside MTPI and the derived NSPI.
Interpretation Different trend horizons can disagree. That disagreement is information rather than automatically a model failure.

Trend describes direction and persistence, not certainty.
