The model

What LTPI measures

LTPI is AstravaQuant's long-term crypto trend model. It combines the total crypto market capitalization with the Bitcoin chart on a weekly timeframe.

The slower timeframe is intended to define structural direction rather than the faster participation state captured by MTPI.

Current state

Weekly structure defines the slow regime

The latest verified LTPI value and workbook state appear in the live snapshot above. Its score is interpreted through the shared crypto regime scale.

LTPI does not erase the faster MTPI reading. Their agreement or disagreement is captured transparently in the derived NSPI rather than reduced to a single unsupported narrative.
