What MTPI measures

MTPI is AstravaQuant’s medium-term crypto trend model. It aggregates the total crypto market capitalization and TOTAL2 on a five-day horizon.

Total market capitalization captures the broad crypto tape. TOTAL2 removes Bitcoin, adding context on participation outside the largest asset.

How to read it

The score should be interpreted through the shared crypto regime scale rather than as a standalone trade instruction. MTPI is the faster horizon in the public trend stack.

Its interaction with LTPI matters. Agreement and disagreement across horizons are both informative.

Interpretation Fast participation should be compared with slower structure before drawing a regime conclusion.

Disclosure boundary

The public repo discloses the input universe, normalization range, interpretation bands, source tabs, and dated forward-testing history. It does not yet disclose the full formula or parameter register.
